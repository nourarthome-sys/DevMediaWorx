Home/Process

From first call to launch day

One project lead from start to finish. Every stage ends with something you can see and approve before we move on.

01
STAGE 01 · DISCOVERY

We learn your business

A call to understand the business, the audience, the goal and the deadline. We look at what you have today and what competitors are doing.

You receive
  • Written scope of work
  • Fixed price and payment schedule
  • Timeline with milestones
02
STAGE 02 · DESIGN

You see it before we build it

Wireframes and visual design for the website or app. Scripts, mood boards and storyboards for any video. You comment, we revise.

You receive
  • Clickable design prototype
  • Video script and storyboard
  • Approval before build starts
03
STAGE 03 · BUILD & SHOOT

Development and production, side by side

Developers build while the crew shoots, so the footage lands in the product and the campaign at the same time. You follow progress on a live staging link.

You receive
  • Staging link to review anytime
  • Weekly progress update
  • Rough cut for feedback
04
STAGE 04 · LAUNCH

Live, tested and tracked

We test on real devices, connect analytics, pixels and conversion tracking, then go live. Ads and content launch on the same day.

You receive
  • Live website or app
  • Final video files in every format
  • Tracking verified end to end
05
STAGE 05 · GROW

Improve with real numbers

We review what brings leads and what doesn't, and adjust the site, the content and the ads. Or we hand everything over with training.

You receive
  • Monthly report in plain language
  • Recommendations for next month
  • Full handover if you prefer
